Past Masters

This list is taken from the Past Masters archive on the Officers page. Each year and name is copied from that archive. Where the archive names no Worshipful Master, the year is marked “Not listed.” No names have been added.

The archive has no Worshipful Master for 2018–2019, 2019–2020, 2020–2021, 2021–2022, 2022–2023, 2023–2024, 2024–2025, or 2025–2026. From 1843 through 2017–2018, every year in the archive is named.

The 2026–27 officer roster names W. Bro. Wesley Thompson as Worshipful Master. That roster is the current year, so he is not listed here as a Past Master.
